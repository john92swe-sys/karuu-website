# KARUU Product Marketing Context

Version: 1.2.0
Updated: 2026-08-27

## Positioning

KARUU is a Nordic B2B Active Lifestyle Product Development & Sourcing Partner. Activewear remains the primary product pillar and the source of KARUU's specialist market positioning.

Women’s activewear, yoga, and fitness are the only publicly promoted categories. Hydration & Drinkware remains preserved internally but is temporarily excluded from public discovery until approved high-resolution product photography is available.

## Business model

- B2B OEM / ODM / private-label product development and sourcing coordination.
- Primary buyers: activewear, yoga, fitness, and wellness brands, plus relevant retailers and branded-program buyers.
- Primary conversion: request a sample or request a product-specific quote.
- Consumer checkout, public stock claims, and public retail pricing are out of scope.

## Core narrative

Develop women’s activewear, yoga, and fitness collections with clear sourcing, product development, and OEM/ODM coordination.

## Evidence rules

- Publish only specifications supported by product source files.
- Omit or confirm by inquiry when MOQ, lead time, sample terms, certification, performance, or customization details are not documented.
- Never expose supplier identity, supplier model numbers, source pricing, contact details, or internal factory information.
- Never describe sourced products as original KARUU designs unless documentary evidence supports that statement.
