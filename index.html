About Us

Your Trusted Nordic Trade Partner

Swedish-Registered. Nordic-Proven.

KARUU AB is a Swedish-based cross-border trade company, officially registered in Sweden with full commercial qualifications. We serve as the most professional, stable, and practical trade platform bridging China and the Nordic market.

As the premier cross-border e-commerce platform in Scandinavia, KARUU specializes in providing comprehensive trade solutions for European and Chinese businesses seeking to expand across borders.

Our Mission

"To be the most trusted bridge connecting quality Chinese products with the Nordic market, delivering value to both suppliers and consumers through innovation, efficiency, and local expertise."
